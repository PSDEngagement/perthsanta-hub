Guide to interacting on Facebook

Posts from GMMTV, Brands & Media: When you see posts related to PerthSanta’s projects, jobs, events, or collaborations, actively interact with the posts to help boost their media performance and visibility.

  • 👀 View: Open and watch/read the post or video.
  • ❤️ React: Leave a reaction that matches the content.
  • 💬 Comment: Leave meaningful and relevant comments about PerthSanta or the project.
  • ↩️ Reply: Join conversations and reply naturally to other comments.
  • 🔄 Share: Share the post to your profile, Story, or relevant groups when appropriate.
  • 🔖 Save: Save content you genuinely want to revisit.
  • 🔗 Click: Open relevant links or related content when interested.

Important: Keep interactions natural and relevant. Avoid repetitive or copy-pasted comments and excessive spam. The goal is to create genuine engagement that helps PerthSanta’s projects receive more visibility and attention.
